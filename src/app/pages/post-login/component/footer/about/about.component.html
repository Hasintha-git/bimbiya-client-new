Premium bites and beverages crafted with care for every celebration.

Bimbiya (Pvt) Ltd is a Sri Lankan-owned food service company specializing in high-quality bites and beverages for all types of events. We are committed to delivering fresh, hygienic, and delicious food that brings joy to every celebration.

Founded with a passion for food and customer service, Bimbiya has quickly grown into a trusted name in the catering industry. From devilled bites to seafood platters, our diverse menu is designed to satisfy a wide range of tastes and preferences.

We Cater To

  • Private events (birthdays, weddings, anniversaries)
  • Corporate functions
  • Holiday parties & family gatherings
  • Friendship parties, casual get-togethers, and everyday snacking

Our professional kitchen team ensures every order is prepared to the highest standards of taste and safety. We offer online ordering, WhatsApp support, and timely delivery to make your event planning easier.
